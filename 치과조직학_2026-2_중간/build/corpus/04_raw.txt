Blood-Brain Barrier (BBB)

- highly selective semipermeable border of endothelial
cells
- tight junctions between the endothelial cell
- continuous-type capillaries (including Basal lamina)
• Functions of BBB
- restricts passage of certain ions and substances
(MW < 500 dalton) from the bloodstream to
tissues of the CNS.

electrolytes, hormones, and tissue metabolites
circulating in the blood vessels

- induction of tight junction formation
- maintenance, functional regulation and the repair of
the blood-brain barrier.
