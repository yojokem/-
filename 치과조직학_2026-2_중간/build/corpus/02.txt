Brown Adipose (Fat) Cell

       Loose (Areolar) Connective Tissue

- comprises all the main components of CT proper.
  ; cellular component > fibrous component
  ; the most numerous cell - fibroblast, macrophage
- fills spaces between fibers and muscle sheaths,
- supports epithelial tissue,
- forms a layer that ensheathes the lymphatic and blood vessels.
- papillary layer of the dermis, hypodermis,
  serosal linings of perotoneal and pleural cavities,
  glands, mucous membranes supporting the epithelial cells
  (subepithelial layer, subendothelial layer, lamina propria)
- very well vascularized, not very resistant to stress.

Adipose Tissue

 • White Adipose Tissue
  - storage of energy, cushioning or shock-absorbing.
  - In infants and young children; panniculus adiposus,
       a continuous subcutaneous layer over the whole body.
  - In adults; subcutaneous tissue
  - has a rich network of blood vessels.
  - regulated by hormone (insulin) and autonomic nerve

 • Brown Adipose Tissue
  - is a special type of adipose tissue for heat production.
  - Newborn Animals, Hibernate animals.
    In rodents; interscapular space, shoulder girdle, axillae.
  - extremely rich blood supply.
  - innervated by sympathetic adrenergic neurons.
