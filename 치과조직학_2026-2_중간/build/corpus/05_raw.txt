Immune system
• Defense: protection self from non-self
• self : organism's own molecule
• non-self : microorganism, foreign body, foreign cells, tumor cell

Immune system
• first line of defense
• epithelial barrier
• skin and mucosa
• Stratification, cell junction, keratinization, secretion of mucus and surfactant
• forms a complete lining and covering of the body surfaces.
• second and the third lines of defense
• Immune system
• activated when the physical barrier is breached by a cut, tear, or abrasion,
• even if foreign substances are able to penetrate, but have not yet penetrated,
the intact barrier
• innate immune system
• natural immune system, nonspecific and rapid response
• No immunologic memory
• adaptive immune system
• acquired immune system,
• Specific against antigen, Slower than innate response
• Immunologic memory
